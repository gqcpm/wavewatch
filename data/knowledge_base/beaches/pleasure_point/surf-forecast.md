Pleasure Point-Insides Surf Guide

Pleasure Point-Insides in Santa Cruz is a fairly exposed reef break that has fairly consistent surf and can work at any time of the year. Offshore winds blow from the northwest. Clean groundswells prevail and the ideal swell angle is from the southwest. A right hand reef break. Best around low tide when the tide is rising. Likely to be crowded if working. Beware of sharks ,rocks and rips.
