Please Point is the place I call home. Pleasure Point is an area on the east side of Santa Cruz right up the hill from Capitola Village on East Cliff drive and goes back into the neighborhood behind the street. East Cliff is a street that goes along the edge of the cliffs, so as you drive by you can look out at the water and check the waves. The Point is the most popular spot in this area. It is a surf spot that is more for people that know how to surf and are pretty good. Theres a couple different peaks to surf at, the best one Sewer peak. At the Point theres also 1st peak and 2nd peak. This is not a place that people go to for laying on the beach, but people often hangout on the cliff to watch the waves and get some food at the Point Market.

Going down the line from there, the next spot on Pleasure Point is the Dirt Farm also known as 38th. The Dirt Farm is an open area on the cliffs where often dogs run around and play and it is right above the 38th surf spot. The 38th surf spot is a super mellow wave that is perfect for beginners. People often learn how to surf there or will take out there Wavestorm, Costco soft top board, and mess around with there friends.

Next, is 38th where people lay on the beach. Once you go down the stairs, you can go to the right or left. When you go to the right it is normally local families with their small kids and parents taking turns watching them and going surfing. From the stairs going to the left, is the area where a lot of local teens and early twenty year olds hangout. Walking around there you will see beer bottles, people smoking weed, a lot of towels pulled together, lawn chairs, and dogs running around.

Last on Pleasure Point is the Hook. The Hook is another surf spot. This spot is for people that know how to surf but are not super good. This wave also breaks differently than the Point, so depending on what someone is use to that could make it easier or harder. People do not normally lay on the beach here but it is possible.

My favorite part about Pleasure Point is when it is a super low tide. On a super low tide, all of the tide pools get exposed so people can walk around and look at all the starfish, sea anemones, barnacles, muscles, and seaweed thats in the water. At this time people also bring their dogs down to the beach and let them run around and play with other dogs.

My favorite is in the winter because the city has to dredge the Harbor which means pump sand out of it so boats can get in and out and so there is not as much of a wave. But all the sand that is pumped out moves down the coast and settles on Pleasure Point normally. This makes the beaches bigger and makes the super low tides even lower. The combination of all of this makes Pleasure Point the perfect spot for a sunset walk on the beach.
